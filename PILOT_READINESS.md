# Project A pilot release gate

Reviewed October 8, 2026. Decision: suitable for a labeled research demonstration; not yet ready for a broad local-opportunity pilot across arbitrary markets. No audience invitation has been sent.

## Geographic verification
The reference contains 32,350 Census places across 50 states, DC and Puerto Rico. Automated checks exercised every coordinate and place ID, representative mainland markets, Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, boundary distances, unresolved/ambiguous geography, malformed postal responses and restored preferences. Honolulu's commonly disclosed name now resolves to Urban Honolulu CDP, with East Honolulu kept separate.

Nine real postal lookups returned HTTP 200: Charleston 29412, Cincinnati 45202, Philadelphia 19103, New York 10001, Los Angeles 90012, Seattle 98101, Anchorage 99501, Honolulu 96813 and San Juan 00901. Lookup functionality is not prospect coverage. International prospect discovery and non-U.S. postal search remain unconnected.

The current 73-record catalog has 71 SC and two OH source-disclosed filing/company locations. These are not verified home addresses. Radius checks found 17 reviewed records within 50 miles of Charleston and two of Cincinnati; none in the other seven test markets. Counts include monitoring records, not qualified opportunities. The market panel separately reports reviewed planning-trigger counts and never treats an empty market as proof that opportunities do not exist there.

## Before the pilot group sees it
1. Identify each tester's prospect market and scope; choose local or remote research explicitly.
2. Retain and review enough relevant dossiers for each market to evaluate at least five substantive cases. Automated postal coverage cannot substitute for evidence ingestion and review.
3. Reconcile relevant issuer amendments or keep affected combinations blocked. Confirm primary sources, identities, status and dates in a sample of each market.
4. Verify desktop and phone paths for market selection, source inspection, research brief, watchlist backup and export. Preserve UNKNOWN and future compensation conditions.
5. Complete and document code/source rights and security review before a broader audience, including the previously reported credential alert. A current-source scan is not proof an exposed credential has been revoked.
6. Obtain actual advisor evaluations; separate QA from self-reported advisor feedback. Do not claim usefulness, demand, retained customers or acquisition readiness without that evidence.

## Ideas awaiting a product decision
- Trigger-based conversation drafts: factual opening, permission-based questions and a source-linked explanation; no asserted wealth, received funds, unmet need or automated outreach. Existing research packets provide a foundation, but this feature has not been added.
- FINRA-assisted advisor succession research: BrokerCheck can corroborate registration/employment history. A registration ending is not proof of retirement, a saleable practice or client assets available to transfer. Require a separate explicit primary retirement/succession announcement, exact CRD identity, permitted commercial use and retained evidence. No FINRA feed or retirement detector has been connected.
