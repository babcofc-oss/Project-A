Clear boundaries make the research usable.

The current product combines configured nationwide SEC Form 4 discovery with separately reviewed leadership, business-exit and contact sources. It does not provide exhaustive coverage of every event or location, verified investable assets, automated outreach, account synchronization or a native CRM connection. Contact publication does not establish reachability or consent. Watchlists, review baselines and feedback are stored on each browser; exports provide manual portability.

Gross stock-sale value is not net proceeds. Proposed sales are not completed sales. Current role, advisor relationships, available funds and willingness to engage remain unknown unless separately verified.
