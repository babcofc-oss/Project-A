  • Will the firm pay for continued delivery at an agreed scope and price?

The public research terminal is available now. Firm setup, delivery scope and pilot pricing are agreed directly. No subscription or charge is created on this page.

Clear boundaries make the research usable.

The current pilot combines nationwide SEC Form 4 discovery with separately reviewed leadership, business-exit and contact sources. It does not provide exhaustive coverage of every event or location, verified investable assets, automated outreach, account synchronization or a native CRM connection. Contact publication does not establish reachability or consent. Watchlists, review baselines and feedback are stored on each browser; exports provide manual portability.

Gross stock-sale value is not net proceeds. Proposed sales are not completed sales. Current role, advisor relationships, available funds and willingness to engage remain unknown unless separately verified.
