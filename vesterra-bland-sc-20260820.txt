header-3">Bland Landscaping Completes Two Strategic Acquisitions in South Carolina</h1><div class="base-markdown mb-md intro"><p><strong>West Palm Beach, Fla. –</strong> Vesterra Capital Partners (“Vesterra”), formerly known as Comvest Private Equity, is pleased to announce that its portfolio investment, Bland Landscaping (&quot;Bland&quot;), has completed two strategic acquisitions in South Carolina: Charleston Grounds Management (&quot;CGM&quot;) and Clear Lakes and Wetland Services (&quot;CLAWS&quot;).</p>
</div><div class="base-markdown"><p>Bland is a leading provider of commercial landscaping services in the Southeast, with 17 branch locations. The acquisitions of CGM and CLAWS extend Bland&#39;s footprint in the Southeast within landscaping and aquatics services.</p>
<p>CGM was founded by Lee Hickman and provides landscaping services to commercial customers in Charleston with a focus on HOA, townhome, and multifamily customers. Service offerings include landscape maintenance, enhancements, irrigation, flowers, and aquatics services.</p>
<p>CLAWS was founded by Ken Robinson and provides comprehensive aquatics services to commercial customers in Myrtle Beach, primarily focused on HOA customers. Service offerings include lake and pond management, fountain sales and maintenance, and fish stocking.</p>
<p>&quot;We are excited to expand our landscaping and aquatics capabilities in Charleston and Myrtle Beach, which are key growth markets for Bland,&quot; said Kurt Bland, Chief Executive Officer of Bland. &quot;Lee and Ken have built respected businesses with strong reputations for quality, and we are excited to welcome CGM and CLAWS to the Bland team as part of our broader Southeast platform.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;These acquisitions reflect Bland&#39;s success partnering with established, founder-led businesses in great markets,&quot; said Matt Kunz, Partner at Vesterra. &quot;CGM and CLAWS strengthen Bland&#39;s position in South Carolina and further enhance Bland&#39;s ability to serve customers across the Southeast.&quot;</p>
<p>About Charleston Grounds Management<br>Charleston Grounds Management is a provider of landscape management services to commercial customers in the Charleston, South Carolina area.</p>
<p>About Clear Lakes and Wetland Services<br>Clear Lakes and Wetland Services is a provider of aquatics services to commercial customers in the Myrtle Beach, South Carolina area.</p>
<p><strong>About Bland Landscaping</strong><br>Bland Landscaping is a regional commercial and estate landscape management and contracting business headquartered in Apex, N.C. Best known for its horticultural landscaping and grounds management work, Bland also performs contract care of memorial gardens, parks, and commercial grounds alongside stormwater and pond management services. Led by a team of seasoned industry leaders, Bland has been recognized with numerous industry awards and noted for its commitment to the industry and to local communities. Founded in 2000, Vesterra partners with founders, family owners, and operators who are ready for their next stage of growth. The firm works side by side with management teams providing practical support, targeted resources, and strategic guidance shaped around each business. Headquartered in West Palm Beach, Florida, Vesterra manages approximately $1.9 billion in assets. For more information, please visit <a href="https://vesterracap.com" >https://vesterracap.com</a>.</p>
